Pakkit — Privacy

Pakkit sends the APK you choose directly from your browser to your Wear OS watch over Wi-Fi. There are no accounts, analytics, ads, cloud storage or remote services in Pakkit.

Your connection

Your watch asks before allowing a browser to connect. Access ends when you stop receiving or Pakkit’s process ends. Your browser remembers a random access credential in storage for this watch address. You can remove it using your browser’s site settings. An old credential cannot reopen a stopped session. Browser and device names are hints from the browser; your watch also shows the network address.

Use Wi-Fi you trust. Files and access credentials travel without encryption. Someone with access to your network traffic could read or alter them. Browser approval does not encrypt the connection.

Your files

APKs and their package details are saved in private watch storage. Pakkit checks package information and a file fingerprint before installation; it does not certify that an app is safe. Installation always requires your action and Android’s approval. Android may perform its own checks.

Rejected and interrupted uploads are removed. A received file stays until you remove it, replace a failed review, or finish installation. Files left by an interrupted process are removed when Pakkit next starts. Backups of Pakkit’s private files are disabled. Uninstalling Pakkit removes its private files; apps you installed are separate.

Receiving continues until you stop it or your watch ends it. Losing Wi-Fi interrupts a transfer, and receiving resumes when Wi-Fi returns. Stopping receiving does not cancel an installation already handed to Android.
