## App name

Pakkit

## Short description

Send APKs from your browser to your Wear OS watch. Install on your terms.

## Full description

Pack it. Send it. Wear it.

Give your next app a place on your wrist. Pakkit brings APKs from your browser to your Wear OS watch, with a thoughtful experience from the first connection to the final tap.

Open Pakkit, start receiving and scan the code or open the address on any device with a compatible browser. Choose your APK, follow its progress, then review and approve installation on your watch.

• Connect with a quick approval on your watch.
• Send files directly over your Wi-Fi.
• Follow your transfer on both screens.
• Review every app before installation.
• Keep receiving until you’re ready to stop.

Use a trusted Wi-Fi network that lets your devices connect to each other. Transfers use an unencrypted local connection. Choose complete APK files compatible with your watch; installation depends on your watch’s permissions, available storage and Android’s security checks. App bundles and split APK sets are not supported.

Your watch. Your call.
